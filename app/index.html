Roadmap to the platinum

About 200 hours, one playthrough, no online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission replay. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter.

  1. Step 1 — Play the story 60–80 hours

    Chapters 1 to 5, about 60–80 hours. Story trophies unlock on their own. While you play, scan new vehicles with the WAiNK app and study animals through the binoculars — both count toward the 100% checklist later. Do the side strands as they appear. Do not skip the Chapter 1 meditation (see the missable warning below).

  2. Step 2 — The 100% checklist about 100 hours

    After the final mission the checklist opens under Progress → Stats → 100% Completion. It is forgiving — closer to 60% true completion: most side quests are not needed, collectibles are only partly required, and you need any 70 of the 80 challenges. Robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret’s quests are the parts that need everything.

  3. Step 3 — Strangers & cleanup about 10 hours

    Finish the Strangers & Freaks strands, the last challenges and any trophies left over. The platinum, Vice Got a Price!, pops the moment the 54th other trophy does.

Missable trophies

Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy that may be missable. During Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning — she says she is going to meditate, walks to the balcony, and you can follow her and choose Accept. That balcony meditation is gone after Chapter 1. Every other trophy can be earned after the story in free-roam, and every trophy-related quest giver returns after the story.

Tip: keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. There are 15 manual save slots plus one autosave slot.
